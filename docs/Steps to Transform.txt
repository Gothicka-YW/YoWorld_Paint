Steps to Transform

1. Open the Transform tab.
2. Load the preset you want, such as Left Facing or Right Facing.
3. Choose whether to resize to 390 x 260.
4. Use multitile output when the transformed image should cover multiple boards.
5. Browse for an image or use the upload/drop area.
6. Click Apply.
7. Review the preview.
8. Click Export All Tiles.
9. Use the Home tab to upload each exported tile.
10. Apply each tile to its matching YoWorld paint board with the normal Redirect workflow.

Notes

- A transparent source can produce blank or partly blank tiles when the transformed canvas covers more area than the visible artwork.
- If you are working with a large banner or wall design, calculate the board grid first in Tools.
- Side Panel mode is useful for Transform and multiboard work because it gives the extension more room.
